Sale opens 10 days before travel

When can you actually buy this ticket?

Bangladesh Railway only lets you see a date once it is inside the 10-day selling window. Pick any date — even months out — and this tells you the exact day and time its tickets go on sale, which trains run your route, and how many seats are left right now.

      Popular

      Schedules, running days, stop timings and ticket-sale opening times come from Bangladesh Railway's own e-ticketing service. Seat counts and fares are read live from your own signed-in session when you add one in Settings.

      Always complete the purchase on eticket.railway.gov.bd — this site never handles payments or personal data.
